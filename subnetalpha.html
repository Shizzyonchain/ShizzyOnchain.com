About Us

The Ultimate Free Directory for Bittensor Subnets

Welcome to Subnet Alpha.ai, a platform dedicated to sharing comprehensive and up-to-date information about the various subnets within the Bittensor network. Our mission is to provide a transparent and freely accessible resource for everyone interested in the decentralised AI ecosystem. We believe in the power of open information and community collaboration.

Our website is a non-commercial, volunteer-driven project inspired by platforms like Taostats. We aim to facilitate knowledge sharing and foster a deeper understanding of each subnet’s unique contributions, capabilities, and future developments. Here, you will find detailed information about the purpose, team, roadmap, and announcements for each subnet, contributed by members from across the Bittensor network.

We are committed to maintaining a platform free from monetisation, ensuring that our focus remains solely on delivering accurate and valuable content to the community. Join us in exploring and contributing to this vibrant and innovative AI network.

Thank you for being a part of our journey.
